Walpurga Systems

Battery-aware design for Industrial IoT

Battery-aware design is much more than minimizing energy consumption or balancing energy consumed vs energy harvested.

Understanding and optimizing the interaction between node activity and the specific electrochemical dynamics of its energy storage device is the key to obtaining long and predictable device lifetime.

Currently being founded in Uppsala, Sweden, Walpurga Systems builds on research done at Uppsala University in battery-aware design for low-power wireless systems.

Offering

Walpurga Systems provides focused tutorials and seminars on energy storage devices for low-power wireless communication.

Walpurga Systems provides consulting services on battery-aware design for IoT systems.

Experimental studies

Walpurga Systems has expertise to provide large-scale measurement campaigns directed toward optimizing specific protocol and application design choices.

About

Founder Laura Marie Feeney has more than two decades of experience in low-power wireless communication. She led the development of the UU-CoRe Battery Testbed, a unique facility for scalable, application-oriented measurement of small energy storage devices (batteries, capacitors and hybrids).
