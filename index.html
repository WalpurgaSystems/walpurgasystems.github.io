Walpurga Ssytems

Battery-aware design for Industrial IoT

Ensuring long -- and predictable -- lifetime for battery-dependent IoT nodes requires understanding and optimizing the relationship between node activity and the specific electrochemical dynamics of its energy storage device.

Currently being founded in Uppsala, Sweden, Walpurga Systems builds on research done at Uppsala University in battery-aware design for low-power wireless systems.
